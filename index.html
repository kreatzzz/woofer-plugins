What it does

Play here. Take it there.

A real Spotify Connect device on your desktop, with controls for speakers, phones, TVs, and more.

Keep the words close.

Synced lyrics with optional translation and romanization, plus a quiet, honest fallback when words are unavailable.

Light on the room.

A small desktop app that starts quickly, stays keyboard-friendly, and keeps the music going from the tray.

Woofer is extendable

Plugins are sandboxed WebAssembly modules: they compute, Woofer fetches for them and holds each one to the domains it declares, and nothing is listed without a reviewed merge. The app needs none of them — its built-in sources carry every kind — and Open in Woofer hands the install to the running app.
