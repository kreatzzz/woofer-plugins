Woofer

A fast, native Spotify client for macOS, Windows, and Linux. It starts in well under a second, stays small while it runs, and there is no browser engine anywhere in the process.

Download Woofer

macOS · Windows · Linux

Free, MIT licensed. Playing music needs Spotify Premium; browsing works on any account.

What it does

A Connect device

Press play here, or pick Woofer from your phone. Move playback to any speaker and keep controlling it: play, pause, skip, seek, shuffle, repeat, volume.

Finds your receivers

A librespot, spotifyd, or hardware receiver is invisible to Spotify until it has an account. Woofer discovers one on your network over mDNS and connects it.

Your whole library

Playlists, Liked Songs, saved albums, followed artists, podcasts. Playlists you own are editable; the sidebar pins, filters, and takes your order.

Search everywhere

Songs, artists, albums, playlists, podcasts, and episodes, with a top result and per-type views.

Synced lyrics

Line-by-line lyrics that follow the song; click a line to seek. Translation and romanization are one toggle away.

Album-art colour

Pages and the player bar take a tint from the cover you are looking at or listening to. Light and dark, or follow the system.

Stays in the tray

Closing the window keeps the music playing; the tray brings the window back. MPRIS and your media keys see it like any other player.

Keyboard-first

Every common action has a shortcut, and Ctrl+/ lists them all.

A plugin sandbox

One .wasm file computes; Woofer fetches, caches, and enforces its declared domains. A plugin decorates the app — it never becomes it.

Woofer is extendable

Plugins are sandboxed WebAssembly modules: they compute, Woofer fetches for them and holds each one to the domains it declares, and nothing is listed without a reviewed merge. Translate and Romanize ship inside the app already; the catalog adds more. Open in Woofer hands the install to the running app.
